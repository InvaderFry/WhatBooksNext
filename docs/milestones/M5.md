# Milestone 5: all modes and taste

**Status: built and checked in the browser.** Every M5 feature in PLAN.md §18 is in place, with
sentence-style tests, the demo populating every mode, and screenshots inspected. Nothing in M5 is

## What was built

### The ten modes (§13.10) — `server/src/lib/modes.ts`

All modes start from one **shared pool**: eligibility (§13.1), one card per book (§11.3 rule 4),
the filters (kind, Ready to read, Complete stories only, a topic), then **one per series**. No
mode loosens it to fill the screen; an empty list says why, in plain words.

| Mode                  | Rule as built                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Read next             | Unchanged from M3, now with Tune weights and taste.                                                                                                                                     |
| Continue reading      | In progress. Shortlisted first in shortlist order, then Nearly done → Halfway → Into it → Sampled, most recently touched first. Ignores the time control.                               |
| Next in series        | The pool's next installment of every series with something finished or in progress (a volume read elsewhere counts). In-progress first, then the most recently active series.           |
| One-sitting reads     | Finish-within with the picked time, or 2 h when "Any". Only measured fits; rough ones under "Might fit". The scope says installment or whole book.                                      |
| Waiting on your shelf | Highly rated, not started. Oldest acquired date first, ties by rating; unknown dates last, labelled "when you got it is unknown". The date says where it came from.                     |
| Hidden gems           | Highly rated, a vote count in the bottom 25% for its source here, and at least 5. Unknown counts and series-scope ratings never count.                                                  |
| Classics you missed   | Highly rated, never started (no start date either), first published 25+ years ago. The rule is on screen.                                                                               |
| Still relevant?       | Tech books not started and not decided (Want / Never / reference). Version-specific titles first, then outside the topic interests, then oldest. "Decide later" goes last.              |
| Mood                  | Escape / Learn / Skim add up to 0.3 to matching items' scores. Skim = whole length ≤ 60 min, or reference. Reference items appear only here (Skim), in a topic, and in Still relevant?. |
| Surprise me           | One card from the top 50 of Read next, probability ∝ score². Never repeats the previous pick straight away. **Not today** hides it for the tab's session.                               |

- **Version-specific titles** come from a list of technologies followed by a version
  ("React 16", "Python 3.12", "HTML5", "Visual Studio 2010"). Names that are ordinary words
  ("Word", "Go", "Access", "Office") count only in the year form or written together ("ES6").
  "5th Edition" is never a version.
- **Signals, not verdicts:** Still relevant? shows "Names a version", "Published 1985 (41 years
  ago)", "About X, one of your topics" / "Not about any of your topics", "Looks like a reference
  book". No word like stale or outdated, and age is no penalty anywhere (scenario 9).
- **Complete stories only (§13.3):** standalone works pass; a series passes only when a source or
  the owner says it's finished, its length is stated, and every number from 1 is owned or read.
  Unknown fails. With the filter on, a line counts what was left out and why (story ongoing,
  installments missing, not known to be finished). Cards already show "story ongoing" and gaps.
- **Topic interests** (Settings): up to 30, matched as whole words against the title and the
  subjects and tags from sources and the file. The **Topic** filter on What next shows one topic,
  reference included.

### Taste learning (§13.11) — `server/src/lib/taste.ts`

- **Signals:** genres and tags (split from source subjects; words like "Fiction", "General",
  "Large type books" dropped), authors and comic writers, comic artists (by role), publishers
  (from the file), and kinds.
- **Evidence:** your rating (value from −1 to 1, weight 1) > finished without a rating (value +0.5,
  weight 0.6) > imported history (half weight). Among unrated outcomes only DNF "Not for me" is
  negative (value −1); "Not now" and "Too difficult" teach nothing (scenario 7).
- **Standing out from your own average:** each signal's mean is shrunk toward your overall mean
  with two books' worth of prior, and needs at least one book of evidence.
- **Series cap:** a series counts as at most 2 outcomes; its items share that weight.
- **Inert below the threshold:** 10 outcomes after the cap, from 3 different authors or series.
  Until then the taste component is exactly neutral for everyone.
- **Strength:** Off / Low / Normal / High multiply the effect (0, 0.5, 1, 2).
- **Reasons** name the most specific signal that stands out ("You rate books by … highly" before
  "You rate fiction highly"), and "You finish …" when it's finishing rather than rating.
- **Tune** (`/?view=tune`, from Settings): the six weights with plain explanations, Save and
  Reset; the strength; what taste has learned and what it counts against. Weights, strength and
  topics are owner data in `settings`, so backups carry them (tested). Reset leaves the strength.

### Triage, Still reading? and the rest

- **Triage sources:** everything, **new arrivals** (items first seen after their folder's first
  scan — a new format of a known book joins the known item, so it never counts), or one folder.
  After a scan finds new items, What next offers "12 new items since the last scan — triage
  them?" once per scan.
- **Bulk triage:** tick books, choose Already read (date unknown) or Never, see a preview (what's
  already so, progress it replaces, shortlisted books), apply, then Undo. Undo puts back the exact
  earlier fields and leaves alone any book edited since. The undo record lives in memory (last
  10), so it doesn't survive a restart.
- **Still reading? (§13.6):** in progress, last touched 90+ days ago, not snoozed. One banner on
  What next and one digest at the top of Continue reading, never separate prompts. Still reading
  (resets the clock), Snooze 30 days, Back to not started, Did not finish (with the optional
  reason). A book with no record of when it was touched isn't guessed at.
- **"Not this, because…"** now logs which mode the card was in.

### New starting values (`config.ts`, each with its reason)

From §20: hidden gems (bottom 25%, ≥ 5 votes), classics (25 years), one-sitting (2 h), Surprise
me (top 50, score²), taste threshold (10 outcomes, 3 groups), series cap (2), Still reading?
(90 / 30 days). Assumptions added here, to tune with use: mood weight 0.3, Skim ≤ 60 minutes,
evidence weights (1 / 0.6 / ×0.5 imported), the shrinkage prior (2 books), minimum signal (1 book),
reason threshold (0.15), strength multipliers.

### Demo

Every mode is populated: an invented reading history (ten books read elsewhere, three by the
author of an owned book, a few self-help books rated low, one "Not for me") switches taste on and
lifts that author's owned book; an invented "Widget Recipes for Python 2" leads Still relevant?;
two books have acquired dates for Waiting on your shelf; one book stalled for 130 days fills
Still reading?. Empty cases (e.g. Classics with Comics and manga) say why they're empty.

  is too strong or too weak.

  so they sort last, labelled unknown. There's no field yet for entering when you got a book;
  it's worth adding if this list turns out mostly undated.
